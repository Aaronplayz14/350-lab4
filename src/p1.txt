Because the column-major fill stores the same rng(0) sequence, 
iterating with columns on the outside visits the same integers in the same order, 
which is why the two sums are equal. 
While the column-major walk is almost 20 times slower since it skips 4000*8 = 32 KiB between successive accesses and misses the cache nearly every time, 
the row-major walk reads memory sequentially and uses the hardware prefetcher.
